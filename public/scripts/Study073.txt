# 건강 부상·골절

I got a paper cut.
A: Ouch! I got a paper cut.
B: Oh, dear! That looks nasty!

I've got a splinter in my finger.
A: Ouch! I've got a splinter in my finger.
B: Don't worry. I happen to have a pair of tweezers.

I got scalded.
A: I need ice! Quickly! I got scalded.
B: Here, use this ice from my cup!

I sprained my ankle.
A: Why are you limping all of a sudden?
B: I sprained my ankle. Let's walk slowly.

I pulled a ligament.
A: I pulled a ligament. It will take two weeks to heal.
B: Oh, I see. I'll postpone our ski trip.

I fractured my leg.
A: Why weren't you at school today?
B: I fractured my leg. Oh, yeah! What's our homework?

I got a cast on my arm.
A: I got a cast on my arm. Do you like the color?
B: What? You colored it pink!

What's with the plaster cast?
A: What's with the plaster cast?
B: I slipped on some ice yesterday.

I have a hairline fracture on my tailbone.
A: Don't be shy and sit down.
B: I can't. I have a hairline fracture on my tailbone.

I hope it doesn't leave a scar.
A: Look at my chin. I got ten stitches.
B: I hope it doesn't leave a scar.
