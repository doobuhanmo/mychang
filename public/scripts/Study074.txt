# 건강 치아 & 눈

I think it's a cavity.
A: It aches so much. I think it's a cavity.
B: Let me take a look. Open wide.

My gums hurt.
A: My gums hurt. What should I do?
B: I recommend this toothpaste. Try it.

I got a root canal.
A: Why do you keep wincing? Are you in pain?
B: Yeah. It's just that I got a root canal.

My eyesight is getting worse.
A: My eyesight is getting worse.
B: Do both of your parents wear glasses?

Your eyes are puffy.
A: Your eyes are puffy. Did he...?
B: Yeah, he dumped me.

My teeth need scaling.
A: Oh, your teeth are brownish.
B: They are, aren't they? I think my teeth need scaling.

I had my tooth taken out.
A: I had my tooth taken out finally.
B: But I loved your snaggletooth!

My wisdom tooth is coming in.
A: My wisdom tooth is coming in.
B: Oh, man! That must hurt a lot.

Your eyes are bloodshot.
A: What's wrong? Your eyes are bloodshot.
B: Really? Oh, it must be my new contact lenses.

I have a sty in my right eye.
A: I have a sty in my right eye.
B: You're telling me this now? After we had lunch?
