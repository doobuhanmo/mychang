# 오랜만 & 안부

Long time no see!
A: Hey! Long time no see!
B: Yeah! It's been five years, right?

You haven't changed a bit.
A: Wow! You haven't changed a bit.
B: I know you're lying. But thanks anyway.

I ran into Alex.
A: Hey, you know what? I ran into Alex.
B: Huh? That can't be. Alex is in India.

It's a small world!
A: James! It's you, right?
B: Hey, Melissa! It's a small world!

What brings you here?
A: What brings you here?
B: Oh, I'm on my honeymoon.

How's everything?
A: How's everything?
B: I'm doing great. I got promoted last month.

How's your health?
A: How's your health?
B: Not so good. I'm a bit worried.

How are your parents?
A: How are your parents? I haven't seen them for ages.
B: They have taken up a new hobby recently. It's salsa dancing.

Give Jane my best.
A: Give Jane my best.
B: Oh, how about calling her yourself? I'll give you her number.

How are you holding up?
A: How are you holding up? I heard you got fired.
B: I'm hanging in there.
