# 외모 나이

I'm in my late twenties.
A: May I ask how old you are?
B: I'm in my late twenties.

I'm pushing thirty.
A: You look down. What's up?
B: I'm pushing thirty. But I don't even have a boyfriend.

I've turned thirty.
A: What are all these presents?
B: It's my birthday today. I've turned thirty.

You haven't aged a bit!
A: You haven't aged a bit! You could pass for twenty.
B: Actually, I tell people I'm in high school.

You have a baby face.
A: You're lucky. You have a baby face. Girls dig that nowadays.
B: Yeah, I know.

You look young for your age.
A: You look young for your age. What's your secret?
B: I sleep ten hours and drink two liters of water a day.

He's four years older than me.
A: How old is your fiancé?
B: He's 34, four years older than me.

We're the same age.
A: My age? I'm 29 years old.
B: Oh! We're the same age.

Age is just a number.
A: Huh? You're learning to skydive at your age?
B: Age is just a number.

I'm feeling my age.
A: Oh, I'm aching all over. I'm feeling my age.
B: Nonsense! You're still young, Aunt Sophie.
