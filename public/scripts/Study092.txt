# 일상생활 거주지 & 출퇴근

I live in a country house.
A: I live in a country house.
B: Lucky you! I'm fed up with the city life.

I live in the suburbs of Seoul.
A: I live in the suburbs of Seoul.
B: I see. So how do you commute to work?

The rent is reasonable.
A: How do you like living here?
B: Well, the rent is reasonable. That's all I care about.

I just moved in.
A: Hey! Why are you following me?
B: Oh, no. I'm your new neighbor. I just moved in.

I leave for work at 8 a.m.
A: I leave for work at 8 a.m.
B: You must live near work. It's 7 o'clock for me.

I leave work exactly on time.
A: I leave work exactly on time every day.
B: Do you think that's wise?

I prefer the subway to the bus.
A: I prefer the subway to the bus.
B: I prefer neither. That's why I drive to work.

It takes 50 minutes to commute.
A: It takes 50 minutes to commute.
B: That's not so bad. I envy you.

It's a 30-minute walk.
A: How long does it take on foot?
B: Umm. It's a 30-minute walk.

I carpool to work.
A: I carpool to work.
B: What a great idea! Can I join, too?
