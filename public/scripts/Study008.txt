# 불만 & 꾸중

It's not fair.
A: Mom, why can't I stay up late like you? It's not fair.
B: Sorry, dear. You are too young.

Stop lecturing.
A: You should be home by 8 p.m. Also—
B: Oh, Mom! Stop lecturing me.

Not again.
A: Come on. Open the Christmas present from Grandma.
B: Let's see... Not again!

There you go again.
A: My friend's son got straight A's again.
B: There you go again. Just leave me alone!

Don't take it out on me.
A: What are you doing? Don't take it out on me.
B: I'm sorry. I've been under a lot of stress lately.

You stay out of this.
A: This is between Mary and me. You stay out of this!
B: Hmph, but I'm Mary's best friend.

Don't give me that.
A: I can't come to work today. My mother is very sick, you see.
B: Don't give me that. Come to work right now.

Stay away from me.
A: I told you I won't date you. Stay away from me from now on.
B: Okay, I won't bother you again.

Don't you dare.
A: I'm going to tell Mom that you cheated on your test!
B: Don't you dare! If you do, I'm going to post your report card on my Facebook.

Don't rub it in.
A: This is the third job you've lost, right?
B: Yes! Don't rub it in.
