# 난관 — 걱정 & 문제 발생

Is everything all right?
A: Is everything all right? You cried out in your sleep.
B: Oh! I had a nightmare.

What's going on?
A: What's with the noise? What's going on?
B: Sorry, Dad. I was just experimenting.

What's the matter with you?
A: What's the matter with you? Are you drunk?
B: Yes, I am. Oh! Did I just break the window?

What's bugging you?
A: What's bugging you? You haven't touched your food.
B: I got a letter from Jason again.

What seems to be the problem?
A: What seems to be the problem? Do you need help?
B: Please, just leave me alone.

Something came up.
A: Where are you going? You haven't finished eating.
B: Something came up. Sorry, see you next time.

I'm in a tight spot.
A: Why haven't you answered my calls?
B: Sorry. I'm in a tight spot right now. I'll call you later.

It got out of hand.
A: You told me it was safe!
B: I know. It got out of hand. Sorry about the fire.

The cat's out of the bag.
A: What should we do? The cat's out of the bag.
B: Actually, I'm glad in a way.

Who spilled the beans?
A: Who spilled the beans? Was it you?
B: No, no. It was my cousin. He read my diary.
