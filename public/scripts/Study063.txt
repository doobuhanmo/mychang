# 외모 고민

I've gained weight.
A: Your shirt is ripped. Have you maybe...?
B: Yeah, I know. I've gained weight.

I've put on some weight.
A: I've put on some weight recently.
B: No, you haven't at all.

I'm extremely obese.
A: I'm extremely obese. Dr. Kim told me to lose at least 5 kgs in a month.
B: Why don't you get liposuction?

I'm losing my hair.
A: Why are you wearing a hat? It's nighttime.
B: To tell you the truth, I'm losing my hair.

My hairline is receding.
A: I'm thinking of wearing a wig. My hairline is receding.
B: You look fine. Don't worry.

My hair is so rough.
A: My hair is so rough. What should I do?
B: In that case, how about trying this shampoo?

I have fine hair.
A: Could you describe your hair for me?
B: I have fine hair. And it keeps falling out.

I'm going gray.
A: Oh, look. I'm going gray.
B: You mean you didn't know?

I have crow's feet.
A: I don't believe it. I have crow's feet.
B: So what? Actually, it suits you.

I'm getting liver spots.
A: I'm getting liver spots nowadays. How do I get rid of them?
B: Oh, why don't you try these pills?
