# 건강 약·접종 & 건강검진

I took some indigestion medicine.
A: How's your stomach?
B: It's okay now. I took some indigestion medicine.

I need some painkillers.
A: I need some painkillers. It hurts so much.
B: But you had one just an hour ago.

I got a flu shot.
A: I got a flu shot. Wow, it hurt.
B: How much did it cost you?

Could you fill this prescription?
A: Could you fill this prescription?
B: Let's see. Oh, we are out of this drug at the moment.

I'm worried about the side effects.
A: Honestly, I'm worried about the side effects.
B: Don't worry. Look at me. I'm just fine!

I need to fast for a day.
A: The doctor told me I need to fast for a day.
B: Do you think you can do that? Oh, let's make a bet!

I had a medical checkup.
A: I had a medical checkup today.
B: When do you get your results? I'm worried about your liver.

I had an x-ray done.
A: I had an x-ray done finally. It didn't hurt a bit.
B: That's what I've been telling you all along!

There's nothing particularly wrong.
A: I got the results back just now. There's nothing particularly wrong.
B: Is that so? Then how about your heart?

My blood pressure is a bit high.
A: Why are you taking that pill?
B: My blood pressure is a bit high. Don't tell my wife.
