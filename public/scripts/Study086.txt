# 문화생활 영화 & 노래

I'm a die-hard fan of action movies.
A: But you've already seen "Die Hard" four times!
B: I can't help it. I'm a die-hard fan of action movies.

That movie is 3 hours long.
A: That movie is 3 hours long. Let's not watch it.
B: But I love horror movies. And it's my birthday today.

This movie is a sequel to "Once."
A: This movie is a sequel to "Once."
B: Oh, really? I loved the first one!

I booked two tickets to "Iron Dog."
A: I booked two tickets to "Iron Dog."
B: Oh, good. I felt like watching an animated movie.

It was so-so.
A: Did you enjoy the movie?
B: It was so-so. I wouldn't recommend it.

I'm tone deaf.
A: I know it's only our second date, but how about karaoke?
B: Sorry, I'm tone deaf.

This is my favorite song.
A: Okay, here we go! Ha, ha! This is my favorite song.
B: Not that song again! I've heard it hundreds of times.

That song sounds familiar.
A: That song sounds familiar. I can't remember the title.
B: Me, neither. I hate it when this happens!

I have a frog in my throat.
A: I can't sing. I have a frog in my throat.
B: I see. I'll get you some warm water, then.

I can't hit the high notes.
A: Come on, let's sing the chorus together!
B: Oh, I can't. I can't hit the high notes.
