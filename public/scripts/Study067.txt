# 뷰티 화장·성형

I've put on some makeup.
A: Who are you? Where's Alicia?
B: It's me, you fool! I've put on some makeup.

You've put on too much makeup.
A: What's this? You've put on too much makeup.
B: Just leave me alone. You sound like my dad.

I ran out of skin toner.
A: I ran out of skin toner. That's why I'm going out to buy some.
B: Oh, wait! I'll get some for your birthday.

Apply blush here.
A: Apply blush here, on my cheek.
B: Don't you think it'll be too bright?

My makeup is not sticking.
A: Let's hurry. We're going to miss the movie.
B: Could you give me five more minutes? My makeup is not sticking today.

You need a makeover.
A: I have a blind date in a week.
B: In that case, you need a makeover.

I'm thinking of going to see an esthetician.
A: My skin feels so rough lately.
B: Me too, I'm thinking of going to see an esthetician.

I'm going to have my nose done.
A: I'm going to have my nose done while I'm on vacation.
B: No, don't even think about it!

She got double eyelid surgery.
A: She got double eyelid surgery last month.
B: Wow, it looks so natural. Do you know where she got it?

She's addicted to plastic surgery.
A: She keeps getting her face done.
B: Too bad. She's addicted to plastic surgery.
