# 휴대폰 소리 & 충전·고장

Put your phone on vibrate.
A: We're inside the theater. Put your phone on vibrate.
B: Don't worry. I've already turned it off.

I put mine on silent.
A: Why isn't your phone ringing?
B: Oh, I forgot. I put mine on silent.

Turn it off.
A: Whose phone is that? Turn it off.
B: Umm… Do I press this?

I'm low on battery.
A: What's the matter?
B: Oh, no. I'm low on battery.

My phone battery is dead.
A: My phone battery is dead. Can I borrow your phone?
B: Okay, but make it short.

I need to recharge my battery.
A: Could you wait a while? I need to recharge my battery.
B: Sure. Take your time.

Where's my charger?
A: Where's my charger? Do you have it?
B: Oh, yes. It's inside my bag.

The battery goes out too quickly.
A: What don't you like about your smartphone?
B: I like the design and all, but the battery goes out too quickly.

My cell phone isn't working.
A: How can I help you, sir?
B: Hello. My cell phone isn't working.

I'm going to get a new smartphone.
A: You seem giddy today. What's up?
B: I'm going to get a new smartphone at last.
