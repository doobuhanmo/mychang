# 대화 — 의논 & 요점 & 협상

Does it show?
A: I've gained weight recently. Does it show?
B: No, not at all. Don't worry about it.

Is it just me?
A: Is it just me? I smell something burning.
B: Oh, my gosh! Your helmet's on fire.

What do you have in mind?
A: I want to buy a new dress. It's my birthday.
B: Okay. What do you have in mind?

Anything out of the ordinary?
A: Anything out of the ordinary?
B: Actually, yes. There's a sudden spike in the chart.

What do you make of it?
A: What do you make of it?
B: It's a bad sign. We should evacuate immediately.

Get to the point.
A: Get to the point. I haven't got all day.
B: Okay. Don't enter the competition. You will just embarrass yourself.

That's not the point!
A: I can't help it if I have to work late into the night.
B: That's not the point! You make me feel lonely!

Let's call it even.
A: I thought I owed you 100 dollars.
B: Let's call it even. I just broke your bicycle.

Take it or leave it.
A: This is all I can offer. Take it or leave it.
B: All right. I'm in.

What's in it for me?
A: You want my help again? What's in it for me?
B: Okay, I'll give you 10 percent of what I earn.
