# 감사 호의 & 감동 감사

You name it.
A: You're really going to buy me anything I want?
B: Yup! You name it.

I'll see what I can do.
A: Is there really no way to get this done? The deadline is tomorrow.
B: Hmm. I'll see what I can do.

I'll pitch in.
A: We're collecting money for Mr. Harris. He's retiring next week.
B: Oh, really? In that case, I'll pitch in.

It will come in handy.
A: Take this with you. It will come in handy in your vampire hunting.
B: Thank you. What is it? Hey! It's garlic.

If you scratch my back, I'll scratch yours.
A: Why are you being so nice to me?
B: Let's just say, if you scratch my back, I'll scratch yours.

It's very touching.
A: I didn't expect you to propose. It's very touching.
B: So, is it a "Yes?"

I'm overwhelmed.
A: Ta-dah! Surprised? Happy wedding anniversary!
B: Oh, my. I'm overwhelmed with joy.

I'll make it up to you.
A: This is the last time. I mean it.
B: Thank you! I'll make it up to you someday. I promise.

Thank you for your comforting words.
A: I'm sure your grandfather is resting in heaven.
B: Thank you for your comforting words.

It's a token of my appreciation.
A: What's this? A wristwatch. But why?
B: Please take it. It's a token of my appreciation.
