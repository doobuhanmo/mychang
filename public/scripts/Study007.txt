# 분노 & 화

I'm so exasperated.
A: Somebody scratched my car again. Aargh! I'm so exasperated.
B: I know who did it.

I lost my temper.
A: Oh, man. I lost my temper again.
B: I don't believe this. It was your job interview!

It makes my blood boil!
A: How can that man go free? It makes my blood boil!
B: I know. He kidnapped a child!

That burns me up.
A: This code is unbreakable. Man! That burns me up.
B: Here, let me try.

How dare you!
A: I want you to leave the organization. You have an hour.
B: How dare you! Is this how you pay me back?

How could you?
A: What! You are going to work for Ben? How could you?
B: I'm sorry. I'll find you a replacement.

She's driving me crazy.
A: Why are you drinking so hard?
B: It's my fiancée. She's driving me crazy.

He threw a tantrum.
A: Why is the meeting room such a mess?
B: Oh, it's our boss. He threw a tantrum.

He's sulking.
A: Why is your brother holed up in his room?
B: He's sulking. Mom took away his phone.

Calm down!
A: Why did you throw your purse at that guy? Calm down!
B: That man stole my smartphone!
