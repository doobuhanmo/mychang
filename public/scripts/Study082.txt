# 날씨 전반적인 날씨 & 공기

The weather is perfect.
A: The weather is perfect for camping. Let's get our things ready.
B: Oh, but our RV has broken down.

What mixed weather!
A: What mixed weather! We'd be fools to go out.
B: Yeah, let's stay home. How about a cup of hot chocolate?

The weather's going to improve.
A: Man, I have a field trip tomorrow.
B: Good news. The weather's going to improve tomorrow.

The weather has gotten better.
A: Thank goodness. The weather has gotten better.
B: Phew. It's goodbye to these cumbersome clothes!

There's a draft.
A: There's a draft. Why is that?
B: It's the way this house was built.

The air is dry.
A: The air is dry. Uh-oh, I wear contact lenses.
B: Why don't you just wear your glasses instead?

It's too cold in here.
A: It's too cold in here. Turn on the heater, please.
B: Just put on a sweater.

It's stuffy in here.
A: It's stuffy in here. Can I open the window?
B: I'm sorry. The people outside don't like cigarette smoke.

It smells moldy.
A: Come here and smell this. It smells moldy.
B: Oh, no! Let's take that leather jacket to the dry cleaners.

I need to get some fresh air.
A: I need to get some fresh air.
B: Yeah, it's so stuffy in here. Let's go for a walk.
