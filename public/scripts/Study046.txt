# 관심 기대 실망 & 신경·관심

I can't wait!
A: We're finally off to Bali tomorrow!
B: I can't wait! It's like a dream.

It was beyond my expectations.
A: How did you like my violin playing, Professor?
B: It was beyond my expectations. I'm proud of you.

It's not up to scratch.
A: Your triple Lutz. It's not up to scratch.
B: Sorry, I'm in a slump nowadays.

It's a total letdown.
A: What was that? It's a total letdown.
B: It's my ankle. I twisted it last night.

Don't bet on it.
A: Do you think he'll propose on my birthday?
B: Don't bet on it.

Never mind.
A: Oh, about the kitchen sink? Never mind. It's not clogged.
B: Okay.

I couldn't care less.
A: Matthew has got a new girlfriend. She's pretty.
B: Hey! I couldn't care less.

I'll keep an eye on her.
A: I'm worried about my little sister. She doesn't talk at all.
B: Don't worry. I'll keep an eye on her.

I'm intrigued.
A: I found an old map up in my attic.
B: I'm intrigued. Where is it?

Let's have a look.
A: My computer isn't working. What's wrong?
B: Let's have a look. Ah, you didn't plug it in.
