# 뷰티 패션

I have nothing to wear.
A: Dear, I have nothing to wear.
B: We went shopping last week!

You're all dressed up!
A: You're all dressed up! What's the occasion?
B: I'm going to visit Diana's parents today.

Your tie is too loud.
A: Your tie is too loud.
B: You're telling me this now? We've left home already!

I wear what's in fashion.
A: What kind of clothing do you prefer?
B: I wear what's in fashion.

That dress looks good on you.
A: Yeah! That dress looks good on you.
B: You think so? Okay, I'll take this one.

You look good in pink.
A: You look good in pink.
B: Thanks. I get that a lot.

You have good taste in clothes.
A: You have good taste in clothes.
B: Does this mean you're hiring me?

She's a sharp dresser.
A: She's a sharp dresser. I envy her.
B: I know. She looks like a celebrity.

You make the clothes look good.
A: You make the clothes look good. No wonder you're a model.
B: Thanks. I work really hard to stay in shape.

Only you can pull that off.
A: Oh my, what's that you're wearing? Man! Only you can pull that off.
B: I know.
