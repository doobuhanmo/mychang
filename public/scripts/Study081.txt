# 날씨 맑고 흐림

It's clear today.
A: What's the weather like?
B: It's clear today. How about a picnic?

The weather is mild.
A: The weather is mild. I shouldn't have worn this sweater.
B: Do you want me to carry it for you?

It's beginning to sprinkle.
A: Oh, no! It's beginning to sprinkle.
B: Then let's just stay in the car and listen to some music.

It's raining on and off.
A: It's raining on and off. Should we head home?
B: Yeah, let's go back while it has stopped raining.

It's only a passing shower.
A: Oh, no! I left my window open at home.
B: It's only a passing shower, so it should be okay.

It's raining cats and dogs.
A: Wow! It's raining cats and dogs.
B: And we are inside a cafe. How romantic!

There's a typhoon coming.
A: Did you hear the news? There's a typhoon coming.
B: Let's stay in the basement like last year.

The wind has died down.
A: Phew! The wind has died down finally.
B: Ha ha! Look at your hair. It's hilarious!

The fog is thick.
A: The fog is thick. Driving will be dangerous.
B: Oh, no. We'll be late for the wedding.

The weather is getting worse.
A: Let's stop playing golf. The weather is getting worse.
B: No, no. I think the weather is rather helping me.
