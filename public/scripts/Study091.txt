# 일상생활 요리 & 식사

It's well-seasoned.
A: Can you taste this for me?
B: Mmm. It's well-seasoned.

This is best served chilled.
A: Isn't this dish too cold?
B: No. This is best served chilled.

Heat it in the microwave.
A: The gas stove isn't working.
B: Heat it in the microwave then.

I've burned it.
A: What's this smell? What happened to the lasagna?
B: Sorry. I've burned it.

I'll set the table.
A: I'll set the table.
B: No, you don't! It's your birthday today. Just relax.

Wipe the table with a dishcloth.
A: Wipe the table with a dishcloth.
B: What, with this? Yuck!

Don't talk with your mouth full.
A: Don't talk with your mouth full.
B: Oops! Sorry. Did I get something in your soup?

Finish your food.
A: Finish your food.
B: But I'm about to throw up!

I'm finished.
A: I'm finished. May I be excused?
B: Wait, what about dessert?

Eat your greens.
A: Eat your greens. It's for your health.
B: If that's the case, why do you smoke, Dad?
