# 휴대폰 통화 & 연결상태

Is this a good time for you?
A: Hi, Victor. Is this a good time for you?
B: Actually, no. I was in the middle of something.

I'll call you back.
A: Was that your doorbell ringing just now?
B: Oh, yes. That must be the pizza delivery. I'll call you back.

Sorry, I pocket dialed you.
A: Why didn't you say anything after calling me?
B: Oh, that? Sorry, I pocket dialed you.

Why are you ignoring my calls?
A: Why are you ignoring my calls?
B: Sorry. Someone stole my smartphone.

I'm getting no service.
A: Oh, no. I'm getting no service.
B: It's because we're in the woods. Mine's not working.

You're breaking up.
A: You're breaking up. Let me call you again.
B: Huh? What did you say?

I'm getting static.
A: Can you repeat that? I'm getting static.
B: I see. Let me call you on my landline.

I'm losing you.
A: What? Darling, I'm losing you. Just hang up, okay?
B: But I have something important to ask you.

I'm getting good reception.
A: Phew! Finally I'm getting good reception. How about you?
B: Loud and clear!

The line went dead.
A: That's strange. The line went dead.
B: Maybe it's the weather. Let me go and open the window.
