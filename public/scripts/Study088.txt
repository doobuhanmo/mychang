# 일상생활 기상 & 잠

Rise and shine!
A: Rise and shine!
B: Mmm… Just five more minutes.

I overslept!
A: I overslept! Why didn't you wake me up?
B: Sorry, you were sound asleep. Are you late?

I'm still half-asleep.
A: Can you help me with my homework?
B: Sure, but bear in mind... I'm still half-asleep.

You have sleep in your eyes.
A: What? Do I have ketchup on my face?
B: No. You have sleep in your eyes.

Did you sleep well?
A: Did you sleep well?
B: No, my dog whined in his sleep.

I slept like a log.
A: Wow, I slept like a log all night.
B: Oh, my! You slept for 12 hours.

I didn't sleep a wink.
A: Did you hear about the burglary?
B: Yeah! I didn't sleep a wink last night.

I had trouble sleeping.
A: I had trouble sleeping last night.
B: What, even though I gave you that sleeping pill?

You talk in your sleep.
A: You talk in your sleep. I actually recorded you.
B: Really? Let's listen to it together!

Sleep tight.
A: Sleep tight, my girl.
B: You too, Dad. Sweet dreams.
