# 놀라움 & 충격

No way!
A: Wow! It turns out that I have an IQ of 180.
B: No way!

I don't believe this.
A: I don't believe this. You're saying your dog ate your essay?
B: Yes, Mr. Thomas.

Are you serious?
A: I've decided to travel the world in eight days.
B: Are you serious?

Who would have known?
A: I can't believe Jackson is the murderer. Who would have known?
B: I know! Such a gentleman.

What the heck?
A: What the heck? I think something dropped on top of my head.
B: Oh! A bird flew overhead.

It took me completely by surprise.
A: Did you expect to get so many votes here?
B: No. It took me completely by surprise.

I'm a little shaken.
A: Are you all right? Do you think you can continue studying?
B: I'm a little shaken. May I go home early?

How can this be?
A: Nobody sent me a reply. How can this be?
B: Well, now you know how they really feel about you.

I'm lost for words.
A: She dumped you for your best friend?
B: Yes, that's right. I'm lost for words.

This can't be happening!
A: You have been cut out of the will.
B: What! This can't be happening! I've been so good to my aunt!
