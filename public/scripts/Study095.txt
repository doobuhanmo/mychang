# 컴퓨터 파일 & 주변기기

Did you save your file?
A: Yes! My report is finished. Let's go out for a drink.
B: Did you save your file?

I accidentally deleted the file.
A: Why did you scream?
B: Oh, my God. I accidentally deleted the file.

I can recover the file.
A: Stop crying. I can recover the file.
B: I must have been drunk.

Did you backup this file?
A: Did you backup this file?
B: Of course. I always make three of them.

Can you print this out for me?
A: Can you print this out for me?
B: Sure. How many copies do you need?

The copy machine is out of order.
A: Why haven't you started the meeting?
B: The copy machine is out of order.

The paper is jammed.
A: Why are you shouting at the copier?
B: The paper is jammed. I think it's doing it on purpose.

My computer froze.
A: Harry, what's up? It's 1 a.m.
B: Help me, man. My computer froze.

My computer is lagging.
A: Oh, no! My computer is lagging.
B: If you're in a hurry, use mine.

My computer has a virus.
A: What are all those pictures on your monitor?
B: It's not what you think! My computer has a virus.
