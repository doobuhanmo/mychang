# 가정 임신 & 자녀

My wife is expecting.
A: Why are you buying me beer?
B: My wife is expecting. I'm going to be a dad!

I have terrible morning sickness.
A: I have terrible morning sickness lately.
B: Oh, dear. Hang in there.

My belly is getting bigger.
A: Look at me. My belly is getting bigger.
B: You look lovely, dear.

The baby is due next month.
A: The baby is due next month.
B: I see! Tell me if you need any help.

I had a natural birth.
A: Did you have a C-section?
B: No. I had a natural birth.

I'm married with two children.
A: Tell me about yourself, Mr. MacDonald.
B: Sure. I'm married with two children.

He's a carbon copy of his dad.
A: Look at him! He's a carbon copy of his dad.
B: Oh, he's so cute!

My son is the apple of my eye.
A: My son is the apple of my eye.
B: I can tell. You must be happy.

It runs in the family.
A: Your sister and you are both left-handed?
B: It runs in the family.

I'm strict with my children.
A: James is very well-mannered, Mrs. Benson.
B: Oh, good. It's because I'm strict with my children.
