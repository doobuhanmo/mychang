# 건강 통증①

I feel dizzy.
A: Hey! Why can't you concentrate?
B: I feel dizzy. What did you say just now?

I'm running a fever.
A: I'm running a fever.
B: I told you not to walk in the rain without an umbrella!

I have a migraine.
A: I have a migraine. I'm going to lie down for a bit.
B: Sure. I'll get you some medicine.

I have a dull headache.
A: I have a dull headache. What should I do?
B: Let's take five. You're not getting a cold, are you?

I have a splitting headache.
A: I have a splitting headache.
B: It's all part of a hangover.

My stomach hurts.
A: Mommy! My stomach hurts.
B: Where exactly? Point to it with your finger for Mommy.

I feel bloated.
A: I feel bloated. Maybe I ate too much.
B: Here. Drink some of this tea.

I feel like throwing up.
A: Why do you keep going to the bathroom?
B: Sorry. It's because I feel like throwing up.

I had diarrhea all night.
A: Darling, I had diarrhea all night.
B: I know. I heard you.

I am constipated.
A: I am constipated. What should I do?
B: You should try eating lots of prunes.
