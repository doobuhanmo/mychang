# 시간 체감시간 & 실제시간

Is that the time?
A: Is that the time?
B: Surprised? It happens when you're playing poker.

Time is crawling.
A: Time is crawling. I hate waiting for connecting flights.
B: Let's watch a movie together on my iPhone.

That cuckoo clock keeps bad time.
A: That cuckoo clock keeps bad time.
B: Oh, Mr. Cuckoo is a bit lazy today.

That clock is three minutes fast.
A: Okay, let's go in.
B: Wait! Not yet. That clock is three minutes fast. He told us to come in exactly at four o'clock.

I mistook the time.
A: Why are you late?
B: I mistook the time.

Do you have the time?
A: Do you have the time?
B: Yes, let me just finish my phone call.

It's twelve o'clock sharp.
A: It's twelve o'clock sharp. Amazing, it keeps perfect time.
B: Yeah, and it's two hundred years old.

It's half past ten.
A: It's half past ten. He's not coming.
B: Let's be patient. It must be the traffic.

It's just past six.
A: I must have dozed off. What time is it now?
B: It's just past six. Did you finish your homework?

It's quarter to five.
A: Wake up! It's quarter to five.
B: Fifteen more minutes. I'll wake up at 5 o'clock.
