# 쇼핑 장보기

I'm going to the grocery store.
A: I'm going to the grocery store. Where are the car keys?
B: I have them. Wait, let me go with you.

We're out of toothpaste.
A: Why did you call? Mommy is driving right now.
B: Mom! We're out of toothpaste.

Do you need anything?
A: It's me, dear. I'm at the supermarket. Do you need anything?
B: What? I'm at the supermarket, too. Oh, I see you!

I was just on my way to the store.
A: I'm peckish. Do we have anything to eat?
B: I was just on my way to the store. You can taste test things over there.

I'll try some.
A: Oh, dumplings! I'll try some.
B: Just a moment. Here are some that are hot.

Check the best-before date.
A: Which brand of milk should we buy?
B: They're all the same. Oh, yeah! Check the best-before date.

Let's buy a bundle.
A: Let's buy a bundle like last time.
B: No, they'll go bad before we can eat them all.

Get me a 6-pack of beer.
A: Oh, I almost forgot. Get me a 6-pack of beer.
B: But Dad! You promised to work on your pot belly.

Is this organic?
A: Is this organic?
B: No. If it was, it would be a lot more expensive.

The meat is in aisle six.
A: Excuse me, where's the turkey?
B: The meat is in aisle six.
