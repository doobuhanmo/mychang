# 음식 식성·취향

I'm a picky eater.
A: I give up. Let's just not eat.
B: Frustrating, huh? I'm a picky eater.

I have a big appetite.
A: You're ordering a hamburger set? We just had pizza!
B: You didn't know? I have a big appetite.

He loves snacking.
A: What's he doing in the kitchen? It's not lunch time yet.
B: Leave him be. He loves snacking.

I have a sweet tooth.
A: What are all these candies in your handbag?
B: I have a sweet tooth. Do you want some?

Raw fish doesn't agree with me.
A: I'd like to treat you at a Japanese restaurant.
B: Sorry. Raw fish doesn't agree with me.

I'm a vegetarian.
A: Here's a sirloin steak I made for you.
B: I'm sorry, but I'm a vegetarian.

I like strong coffee.
A: I like strong coffee. For the aroma.
B: Yeah, and it also helps you to stay awake better.

I usually drink green tea.
A: Do you like to drink coffee?
B: Not really. I usually drink green tea.

I prefer black tea to coffee.
A: How about a cup of coffee?
B: I prefer black tea to coffee. Hope they have it here.

Tea calms my nerves.
A: Why do you drink tea all the time?
B: Oh, it's because tea calms my nerves.
