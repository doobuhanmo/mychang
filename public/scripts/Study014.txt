# 말대꾸 & 항변

Look who's talking!
A: Your hairstyle is terrible!
B: Look who's talking!

Don't give me that baloney.
A: You're saying that you own a sports car? Don't give me that baloney.
B: You don't believe me, huh? I'll show you right now.

Don't change the subject.
A: Oh, it's your birthday next week. What should I get you?
B: Don't change the subject. Who is he?

Don't play dumb with me.
A: I don't know what you are talking about. Honest.
B: Don't play dumb with me. I have proof.

What of it?
A: Did you spill milk on the floor?
B: Yeah. What of it?

What's that got to do with it?
A: It seems you don't exercise a lot.
B: What's that got to do with it? Let's continue talking.

Who do you think you are?
A: Hey! You are taking up the whole camping area. Who do you think you are?
B: Before you get all angry, why don't you try my sausages?

What do you take me for?
A: I didn't know you could cook.
B: What do you take me for? I'm an excellent cook.

On what grounds?
A: You are under arrest, Mr. Roland.
B: What? On what grounds?

So sue me!
A: You said you'd give my money back!
B: So sue me!
