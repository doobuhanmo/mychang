# 뷰티 운동 다이어트

I work out every day.
A: Your biceps are awesome!
B: Thanks. I work out every day.

I've taken up yoga.
A: What do you do for exercise?
B: I've taken up yoga recently.

I don't get enough exercise.
A: Look at that pot belly.
B: I know. I don't get enough exercise.

I go power walking every day.
A: What is your secret to staying in shape?
B: It's simple. I go power walking every day.

I need to burn calories.
A: The bikini season is coming. I need to burn calories quickly.
B: How about running ten miles every day?

I meditate a lot.
A: How come you're so calm all the time?
B: I meditate a lot.

I'm on a diet.
A: All right! Order anything you want.
B: No, thanks. I'm on a diet. I'll just have some water.

I've been skipping dinner.
A: Wow! You've lost a lot of weight! What's your secret?
B: Actually, I've been skipping dinner.

I've lost 5 kilograms.
A: Wow! What happened to you?
B: How do I look? I've lost 5 kilograms.

It's the yo-yo effect.
A: I thought you lost weight? What happened?
B: It's the yo-yo effect.
