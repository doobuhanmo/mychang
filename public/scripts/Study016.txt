# 실수 & 후회

I fell for it.
A: How could you lose 1000 dollars in one day?
B: A man was selling miracle pills. I fell for it.

I made a fool of myself.
A: So you proposed to Laura?
B: I made a fool of myself. She's already married.

I didn't see that coming.
A: Sir? The lady over there sent you her bill.
B: What? I didn't see that coming.

I got carried away.
A: Why are you soaking wet?
B: I jumped into the pond. I'm sorry. I got carried away.

I wasn't thinking straight.
A: Why did you cancel our lunch?
B: Oh, I'm sorry. I wasn't thinking straight.

I owe you an apology.
A: I wasn't thinking straight. I owe you an apology.
B: It's okay. I forgive you.

Sorry I snapped at you.
A: Sorry I snapped at you. I had a fight with my wife.
B: Wait! You have a scratch on your face.

Sorry if I offended you.
A: I don't want to talk about my private life.
B: Oh dear! Sorry if I offended you.

Pardon my bluntness.
A: How could you say those things to me?
B: Pardon my bluntness. But you needed to hear those things.

No hard feelings, I hope.
A: You're leaving already? No hard feelings, I hope.
B: None taken. It's just that I have to pick up my son.
