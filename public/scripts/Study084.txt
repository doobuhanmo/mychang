# 계절 가을 & 겨울

Fall is just around the corner.
A: Fall is just around the corner. What should we do?
B: How about a train trip?

The leaves are changing color.
A: Look at that. The leaves are changing color.
B: It's the magic of Mother Nature.

Let's go leaf peeping.
A: Let's go leaf peeping. It's the perfect time.
B: Okay. I'll pack us a lunch, and you do the driving.

Look at the piles of fallen leaves.
A: Look at the piles of fallen leaves. Should we jump into one?
B: No, I want you to rake the leaves.

I get sentimental in autumn.
A: You're still looking out the window! What's out there?
B: Oh, it's just... I get sentimental in autumn.

Autumn is the perfect season for reading.
A: Why are you taking me to the bookstore?
B: Autumn is the perfect season for reading.

It's snowing big, fat snowflakes.
A: Look! It's snowing big, fat snowflakes.
B: Let's take your dog outside. She'll love it.

Everything is covered in snow.
A: Wow! Everything is covered in snow. Huh? Why are you on your knees?
B: Will you marry me?

It must have snowed all night long.
A: John, come to the window! Do you see that?
B: Wow! It must have snowed all night long.

I get cold easily.
A: What? You're wearing three sweaters?
B: I get cold easily. By the way, do I look fat?
