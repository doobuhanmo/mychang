# 자동차 운전

Follow the GPS.
A: Where are we? Just follow the GPS.
B: Don't you trust me?

It's bumper to bumper.
A: Honey, I'll be late. It's bumper to bumper today.
B: You poor thing! Drive home safely.

Let's take a detour.
A: Let's take a detour. There'll be less traffic.
B: I'll leave it up to you, honey.

We're getting on the highway.
A: We're getting on the highway.
B: We're lost.

Fasten your seatbelt.
A: I see a patrol car! Fasten your seatbelt quickly.
B: Dad, mine's broken.

Pop the trunk.
A: Pop the trunk.
B: You brought more beer? Great!

I got a parking ticket.
A: Why are you late?
B: I got a parking ticket. I was double-parked.

Keep the engine running.
A: Keep the engine running. I'll be back soon.
B: No, that's bad for the environment.

I'd like to use valet parking.
A: I'd like to use valet parking. Please be careful.
B: Of course, ma'am.

My car has been towed.
A: My car has been towed.
B: No, I think it's been stolen!
