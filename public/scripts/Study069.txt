# 건강 컨디션

I have chapped lips.
A: I have chapped lips. It happens to me every winter.
B: I only have lip gloss. Do you want to use it?

I've got a nosebleed.
A: I've got a nosebleed. Do you have a tissue?
B: Sure. Here you go.

I keep sneezing.
A: I keep sneezing.
B: Are you allergic to cats? I have a cat.

I'm prone to allergies.
A: You won't eat my peanut butter sandwich?
B: Sorry, I can't eat peanuts. I'm prone to allergies.

I feel tired all the time.
A: What? You're drinking coffee again?
B: I can't help it. It's because I feel tired all the time.

I'm in great shape.
A: Are you confident about today's marathon?
B: Of course. I'm in great shape.

I'm in poor health.
A: Where are you headed?
B: To the hospital. I'm in poor health.

I'm the health-conscious type.
A: Do you exercise regularly?
B: Yes. I'm the health-conscious type.

I feel melancholy nowadays.
A: I feel melancholy nowadays.
B: How long have you been feeling this way?

My head feels fuzzy.
A: Let's take a break. My head feels fuzzy.
B: I see. How about getting some fresh air?
