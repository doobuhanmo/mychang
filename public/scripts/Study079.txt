# 쇼핑 할인 & 흥정

Meat is on sale today.
A: Wow! Such a long line. What's going on?
B: Oh, that? Meat is on sale today. Let's get in line.

You can get 30% off.
A: Why should I buy this car now?
B: It's because you can get 30% off.

It's buy one, get one free.
A: We don't need two. Take one back.
B: It's buy one, get one free. We have no choice.

Can you give me a discount?
A: I really want to buy this smartphone. Can you give me a discount?
B: No, I'm sorry. I have to make a living, you know.

What's your best price?
A: What's your best price? I'm a regular here, remember?
B: Okay. 75 dollars. Nothing lower.

I'll take it for $50.
A: How much do you have in mind?
B: I'll take it for $50.

Throw in a few more.
A: Here are your mangos.
B: Wait, only ten? Come on. Throw in a few more.

Can I just get three dollars off?
A: This is as low as I can go. I'm sorry.
B: Just a little more! Can I just get three dollars off?

This is a rip-off!
A: That will be 980 dollars.
B: What? This is a rip-off! Don't take me for a fool.

Let's settle for 200 dollars.
A: I can give it to you for 240 dollars. This is my best offer.
B: I don't think so. Let's settle for 200 dollars.
