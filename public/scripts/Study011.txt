# 무례함 & 변명

You have no shame.
A: How could you just copy my report? You have no shame.
B: Sorry, dude. I'll buy you a nice dinner if I get an A.

That was out of line.
A: Hey! That was out of line. Apologize to Linda.
B: OK, I don't know what came over me.

You're making a scene.
A: Stop shouting. You're making a scene.
B: I don't care. Is it me or her?

What have you done now?
A: What have you done now? Alice will never forgive you.
B: I know. I totally screwed up.

Where are your manners?
A: Mom, that man is ugly!
B: Oh, dear. Where are your manners?

What an eyesore.
A: Did you see what that man did just now? What an eyesore.
B: I know! How rude. Let's get out of here.

Don't try to gloss over this.
A: Say, how about a drink? You like vodka, right?
B: Hey! Don't try to gloss over this.

Don't slack off.
A: How about we take a break now?
B: Don't slack off. You went out for a cigarette just a while ago.

Don't make excuses.
A: Can I go home? I have a toothache.
B: Don't make excuses. Give me 30 more sit-ups right now.

That's a lame excuse.
A: Do you believe my story now? It really was an alien that did it!
B: That's a lame excuse. Just say you're sorry.
