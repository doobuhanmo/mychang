# 계절 봄 & 여름

The spring has sprung.
A: Yahoo! The spring has sprung.
B: Yes, I can smell it.

I have spring fever.
A: I have spring fever. That's why I called you.
B: Let's go for a drive. I'll pick you up in an hour.

We're in the middle of a cold snap.
A: We're in the middle of a cold snap. Be careful. You'll catch a cold.
B: I already have.

Spring is in the air.
A: Spring is in the air. Want to go for a walk?
B: What a great idea!

There's a yellow dust warning.
A: There's a yellow dust warning today.
B: Oh, no! I'm wearing contact lenses.

It's scorching hot.
A: It's scorching hot. I badly need a cold beer.
B: Let's go inside. I have some in the fridge.

The heat is unbearable.
A: The heat is unbearable. I feel like fainting.
B: Oh, dear! You might have heatstroke.

There's a tropical night phenomenon.
A: There's a tropical night phenomenon. I can't sleep.
B: Should we go and lie by the brook? It'll be cool.

I'm dying of thirst.
A: I'm dying of thirst. Do you have change?
B: I have plenty. But where's a vending machine?

It's muggy.
A: I hate this kind of weather. It's muggy.
B: Should we stop playing tennis, then?
