# 대화 — 동의 & 동감

Sounds like a plan.
A: How about a cold beer after the sauna?
B: Sounds like a plan.

That makes sense.
A: That's why Sonia dumped him.
B: Ah-ha! That makes sense.

In a way.
A: Do you agree with the terms?
B: In a way.

You could say that.
A: Are all these people your followers?
B: You could say that.

I'll second that.
A: I believe we need more gender equality.
B: I'll second that.

That's what I mean.
A: I can't trust Mary. She's so selfish.
B: That's what I mean.

You can say that again.
A: Tom's late again. He's so lazy.
B: You can say that again.

I know what you mean.
A: I hate Monday mornings.
B: I know what you mean. I dread getting up.

That makes two of us.
A: I wish we could just go home. I'm hungry.
B: That makes two of us. What time is it?

I'm not with you on that.
A: I wish the government would fill up this lake.
B: I'm not with you on that.
