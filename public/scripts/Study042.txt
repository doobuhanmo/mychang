# 부탁 & 허락

I need a favor.
A: I need a favor. Could you translate this for me?
B: Okay. Let's have a look.

Can you give me a hand?
A: Oh, this grocery bag is heavy. Can you give me a hand?
B: Sure, Mom. Wow, you bought two watermelons.

Go easy on me.
A: Wow! That was too fast. Hey, go easy on me.
B: Okay. I'll throw more slowly this time.

Can you make an exception?
A: You're too late. Go away. I'm closing the gate now.
B: Can you make an exception? My name is Elsa.

I hope you don't mind.
A: I'm going to open the window. I hope you don't mind.
B: I do mind! The flies will come in.

Not at all.
A: Do you mind if I take this chair?
B: Not at all. Go ahead.

Be my guest.
A: I'd like to go up on to the stage now.
B: Be my guest. I'll dim the lights for you.

Just this once.
A: Mom! Can I sleep over at Steve's house?
B: All right. Just this once.

You owe me.
A: Thank you for hooking me up with Laura!
B: Remember. You owe me.

On one condition.
A: I'm going to lend you some money, but on one condition.
B: Oh, no! What is it?
