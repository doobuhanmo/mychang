# 데이트 & 연인 관계

Do you want to go out with me?
A: I know this is sudden, but do you want to go out with me?
B: I'm married.

Are you seeing anyone?
A: Just curious... Are you seeing anyone?
B: As a matter of fact, yes. It's your friend Henry.

I'm involved with someone.
A: Care for a drink with me tonight?
B: Sorry. I'm involved with someone already.

Fix me up with someone.
A: It's Valentine's Day soon! Fix me up with someone.
B: Sorry. I don't feel like it.

She's out of your league.
A: Should I ask Jiyeong out?
B: Hey, hey. She's out of your league.

He's husband material.
A: I quite like William. He's husband material.
B: Mom! He already has a girlfriend.

They're an item.
A: Hey, John and Tina are kissing!
B: Oh, didn't you know? They're an item.

We're going steady.
A: Is something wrong between you and Jeff?
B: No. We're going steady.

They're a lovey-dovey couple.
A: There they go again! When will they stop?
B: They're a lovey-dovey couple. Leave them be.

I popped the question.
A: I met up with Kristen last night, and I popped the question.
B: Wow! What did she say?
