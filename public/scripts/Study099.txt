# 가정 가족 & 형제

There are four of us.
A: How many members are there in your family?
B: There are four of us.

I support my family.
A: Does your husband earn a lot of money?
B: I support my family.

My grandmother passed away.
A: I can't come to your party. My grandmother passed away.
B: Oh, dear! When is the funeral?

I have a companion dog.
A: Do you have any kids?
B: No, but I have a companion dog.

I want to start a family.
A: What is your New Year's wish?
B: Actually, I want to start a family.

I'm an only daughter.
A: How many brothers and sisters do you have?
B: I'm an only daughter. Does it show?

I'm the youngest of two.
A: I'm the youngest of two.
B: Really? I thought you were an only child.

We're spaced 2 years apart.
A: That's my older brother. We're spaced 2 years apart.
B: Really? I thought he was your Dad.

We're identical twins.
A: We're identical twins.
B: No need to tell me that.

I get along well with my siblings.
A: Tell me about your family.
B: Well, I get along well with my siblings, and...
