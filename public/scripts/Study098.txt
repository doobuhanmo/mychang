# 경제생활 은행업무 등

I opened up an account.
A: I opened up an account.
B: Good for you! Oh, yeah. What about life insurance?

I opened an installment savings account.
A: I opened an installment savings account recently.
B: I see. When will it mature?

I've made a withdrawal.
A: Darling, why is our bank account empty?
B: I've made a withdrawal.

I transferred the money.
A: I transferred the money to you an hour ago.
B: What? I haven't received it yet.

I took out a loan.
A: I'm setting up my own shop. So I took out a loan.
B: How much is the loan?

I need to use the ATM.
A: I need more cash. I need to use the ATM.
B: No, no. If that's the case, I'll pay.

I'd like to withdraw some money.
A: Excuse me. I'd like to withdraw some money.
B: Yes, sir. Just a moment, please.

I'm overdrawn.
A: So, did you get the money from the bank?
B: I couldn't. I'm overdrawn. What happened to my ten thousand dollars?

My credit card maxed out.
A: Oops. My credit card maxed out.
B: So what do you expect me to do about it?

Break this into 5-dollar bills.
A: Can you break this hundred-dollar bill?
B: Sorry, I need them myself.
