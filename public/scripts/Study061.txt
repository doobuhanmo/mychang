# 식당 식사 & 계산

Let's dig in.
A: Here's pizza, hamburgers, and potato salad. Let's dig in.
B: Yeah, let's. Happy birthday, man!

Please help yourself.
A: Welcome to my housewarming party. Please help yourself.
B: Thank you. Oh, look at all this food!

My mouth is watering.
A: My mouth is watering. Let's order what's on TV.
B: But it's half past eleven at night.

Eat it in one bite.
A: How do you eat this seafood?
B: Eat it in one bite. Like this.

Can I have a bite?
A: Can I have a bite? I've finished mine.
B: Even though I have a cold? Okay, here you go.

Just a sip.
A: No, get away from that whisky bottle!
B: Darn, you caught me. Oh, come on, dear. Just a sip.

Another helping, please.
A: There's plenty of food. So if you want...
B: Really? Another helping, please.

Check, please.
A: Check, please.
B: Here it is. Did you enjoy your meal?

Is this coupon valid?
A: Excuse me. Is this coupon valid?
B: Let's see. Oh, this is not a coupon from our store.

Let's go Dutch.
A: Isn't it your turn to buy lunch?
B: No, I don't think so. Let's go Dutch.
