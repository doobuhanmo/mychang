# 쇼핑 가격·계산 & 교환·환불

How much in total?
A: How much in total?
B: Wow, you've got a lot of beer. That will be $148.

How much do I owe you?
A: This is it, sir.
B: Oh, already? How much do I owe you?

What's the regular price?
A: What's the regular price?
B: $500. So this means you're getting a 20% discount.

How much is the group-buy price?
A: How much is the group-buy price?
B: It depends on the number of people involved.

I'll charge it.
A: How would you like to pay, sir?
B: Let's see. Ah, I'll charge it.

I'll pay in cash.
A: I'll pay in cash. I forgot to bring my credit card.
B: Ah, in that case, I can give you a 5% discount.

I'm going to pay in installments.
A: Oh, the statement is here. Let's see. Arrgh!
B: Don't worry. I'm going to pay in installments.

I'd like a receipt.
A: Wait, I'd like a receipt.
B: Oops, let me look for it in the trash can.

I'd like to exchange this.
A: I'd like to exchange this. It's too small for me.
B: Sure thing. Did you bring your receipt?

I'd like a refund.
A: I'd like a refund, but I lost my receipt.
B: Ooh, it's going to be tricky, then.
