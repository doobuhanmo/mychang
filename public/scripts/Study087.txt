# 공부 공부 & 독서

I skipped class.
A: Aren't you supposed to be at school?
B: I skipped class. By the way, where are we going?

I'm cramming for my test.
A: What are you doing at this hour?
B: I'm cramming for my test, Mom. I'm so sleepy.

I cheated on my test.
A: Why does your teacher want to see me?
B: I cheated on my test.

She passed the entrance test.
A: What's this champagne for?
B: It's for your sister. She passed the entrance test.

He's burning the midnight oil.
A: Larry still hasn't come back from the library?
B: He's burning the midnight oil. He wants to get the scholarship.

I'm a bookworm.
A: What is your hobby? Wrestling?
B: You won't believe this, but I'm a bookworm.

This book is a page-turner.
A: You read that mystery novel overnight?
B: This book is a page-turner. I'll lend it to you.

I'll just leaf through it.
A: How can you read it in an hour?
B: It's okay. I'll just leaf through it.

When is it due back?
A: I'd like to borrow this book. When is it due back?
B: In a week.

How much is the late fee?
A: I'm late in returning this book. How much is the late fee?
B: Oh! It's been due for over a year!
