# 컴퓨터 인터넷 & 게임

I googled it.
A: How do you know so much about my past?
B: I googled it.

I surfed the Internet.
A: What did you do all day?
B: I surfed the internet. My eyes are sore.

The Internet connection is slow.
A: Have you looked it up? What's taking you so long?
B: Sorry! The Internet connection is slow.

I'm getting lots of hits.
A: How's your blog coming along?
B: Great! I'm getting lots of hits.

I'm getting lots of comments.
A: Look. I'm getting lots of comments.
B: Wow, there must be several hundred.

Just ignore the hate comments.
A: How can people write comments like these?
B: There, there. Calm down. Just ignore the hate comments.

That's a paid item.
A: Use that magic sword to kill the monster!
B: That's a paid item. Will you buy it for me?

I cleared the level.
A: Yes! I cleared the level finally!
B: Good for you! Hey, can I take over from now on?

I've been hacked.
A: I've been hacked. Should I call the police?
B: Yes, contact the Cyber Crime Unit.

Reset your password regularly.
A: Reset your password regularly from now on.
B: It's locking the stable door after the horse is stolen.
