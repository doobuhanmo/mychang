# 말버릇 & 말조심

Watch your mouth!
A: Your little sister is pretty. I wish she were my girlfriend.
B: Hey! Watch your mouth!

Mind your language.
A: Daehun! Mind your language.
B: I'm sorry, Ms. Lim. I was out of line.

You take it back.
A: You take it back. How can you say I'm fat?
B: But I'm your doctor.

He has a big mouth.
A: Jason says George and Anna broke up.
B: He has a big mouth! You keep this to yourself.

You're too outspoken.
A: Why did your friend suddenly leave?
B: You just don't say those things. Oh! You're too outspoken.

Stop giving me lip service.
A: You look really good in those clothes.
B: Stop giving me lip service. It won't change my mind.

Don't interrupt.
A: Hey, I wasn't done talking. Don't interrupt.
B: Sorry. I got carried away.

Don't talk back to me.
A: But that's because I was helping John, Mom!
B: Don't talk back to me. I already know there's no such friend.

Don't even bring that up.
A: About the presents... We should give them back to each other.
B: Don't even bring that up. Besides, I don't have them anymore.

He's talking gibberish.
A: Is he drunk? He's talking gibberish.
B: Oh, you mean Norman? No, no. He's like that all the time.
