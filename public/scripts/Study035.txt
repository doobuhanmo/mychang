# 대화 — 생각 & 의견

How's it coming along?
A: I heard you're setting up your own business. How's it coming along?
B: It's going according to plan.

That's close enough.
A: How's my parking? Should I back up more?
B: No, no! That's close enough.

No doubt about it.
A: Are you sure you saw Janet? She was with me.
B: No doubt about it. She even winked at me.

By the looks of it.
A: Is the restaurant out of business?
B: By the looks of it. There's no one inside.

It's obvious.
A: Eric is not picking up his phone.
B: It's obvious that he won't pay up.

It's clear-cut.
A: It's clear-cut. Robert is responsible.
B: Yes, he should apologize.

Give or take.
A: How long will it take? I'm freezing.
B: Fifty minutes, give or take.

Ignorance is bliss.
A: What were you guys talking about? Tell me.
B: No. Ignorance is bliss.

How was I supposed to know?
A: That was the new CEO.
B: How was I supposed to know? I'll go and apologize to him.

I'd like a second opinion.
A: I don't believe the results. I'd like a second opinion.
B: Okay, honey. I do, too.
