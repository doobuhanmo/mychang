# 휴대폰 사진 촬영

Let's take a picture together.
A: I really enjoyed today's gathering.
B: I almost forgot! Let's take a picture together.

Can you take a picture of us?
A: Excuse me. Can you take a picture of us?
B: Oh, sure.

Just touch this.
A: Can you tell me how to use it?
B: Just touch this.

Let's use the selfie stick.
A: I don't think we can both fit in the frame.
B: Don't worry. Let's use the selfie stick.

Keep still.
A: Hurry up with the photograph.
B: Keep still, will you?

Say cheese!
A: You guys are too serious. Say cheese!
B: Just take it, already!

Take one more shot.
A: Are you satisfied? Can I go now?
B: No, wait! Take one more shot.

I was taking a selfie.
A: I was taking a selfie. Do you want to join me?
B: No, thanks. You just keep taking them by yourself.

Here's my proof shot.
A: I've finally been to Paris. Here's my proof shot.
B: Oh, you took it in front of the Eiffel Tower.

Send me that photo via messenger.
A: You'll like your picture.
B: Really? Send me that photo via messenger.
