# 경제생활 벌이 & 경제관념

We're a double-income couple.
A: Do you and your husband both work?
B: Yes. We're a double-income couple.

She's rolling in money.
A: How's your cousin Linda doing?
B: Oh, man! She's rolling in money. Her company went public.

He hit the jackpot.
A: I heard Changsik is investing in stocks?
B: He hit the jackpot!

I'm flat broke.
A: I'm flat broke. Linda, can you lend me some money?
B: I'm sorry to hear that. How much do you need?

I'm not doing well moneywise.
A: Krista, will you invest in my start-up company?
B: Sorry, I'm not doing well moneywise.

She's thrifty.
A: What kind of person is she?
B: She's thrifty. She has ten savings accounts.

She spends money like water.
A: It's not a wise idea to marry Monica. She spends money like water.
B: You should have told me earlier.

He's a penny pincher.
A: He's a penny pincher.
B: I agree. He never buys you lunch.

We need to tighten our belts.
A: I got fired from work and you're still out of work.
B: I know. We need to tighten our belts.

I'm out of pocket money.
A: Dad, I'm out of pocket money.
B: I can't give you as much as last time.
