# 대화 — 선택 & 결심

What's it gonna be?
A: What's it gonna be? Is it me or your dog?
B: That's not a difficult question to answer.

It's up to you.
A: We can take a shortcut, but it's very dangerous. Should we? It's up to you.
B: No. Let's stay on this road.

I've got dibs.
A: So many toys! Oh, I've got dibs on that one!
B: You can have them all, if you like.

You leave me no choice.
A: I won't take your offer.
B: Really? You leave me no choice. I'll have to cut your salary.

I'll leave it to you.
A: I can't decide between the two items. I'll leave it to you.
B: Sure. Don't worry.

There aren't many options.
A: What? Is this it?
B: There aren't many options right now. So let's decide quickly.

I've made up my mind.
A: What should we do?
B: I've made up my mind. Let's not tell her.

I'm having second thoughts.
A: You must be excited about marrying George.
B: To tell you the truth, I'm having second thoughts.

Stop second-guessing yourself.
A: I shouldn't have said that to the interviewer!
B: Stop second-guessing yourself. You'll do better.

I need closure.
A: I have to see Jena. I need closure.
B: I think that's a bad idea. Let her go.
