# 뷰티 헤어

I had my hair done.
A: I had my hair done. Can't you tell the difference?
B: Oh, of course. It suits you.

The bob cut suits you.
A: How do you like my new hairstyle?
B: Wow! The bob cut suits you.

You have thick hair.
A: Wow! You have thick hair.
B: Actually, I'll let you in on a secret of mine.

You dyed your hair red!
A: What's this? You dyed your hair red!
B: Yeah, my boyfriend dumped me.

Just a haircut.
A: Do you want me to dye your hair?
B: No. Just a haircut, please.

Trim it all over.
A: Do you want it done like last time?
B: Yes. Trim it all over, please.

Leave my bangs.
A: Leave my bangs, please. I'm growing them.
B: Of course. Don't worry.

I'd like to get a perm.
A: Excuse me. I'd like to get a perm.
B: Oh, you have to wait an hour, is that okay with you?

Thin out my hair.
A: How would you like your hair done?
B: It's so hot. Thin out my hair, please.

I'd like a scalp massage.
A: There. All done! I hope you like it.
B: Oh, yeah. I'd also like a scalp massage, please.
