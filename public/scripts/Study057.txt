# 음식 맛②

It's fantastic!
A: Is the food to your taste?
B: It's fantastic! I think there's a reason why a lot of people love it.

It was an excellent meal.
A: Wow! It was an excellent meal.
B: I know. I heard the chef has 15 years of experience at the city's most famous hotel restaurant.

It has a good aftertaste.
A: Yummy! What kind of condiments did you use? It has a good aftertaste.
B: My mother-in-law won't tell me.

I don't like the aroma.
A: Why are you covering your nose?
B: It's that exotic food. I don't like the aroma.

The rice is mushy.
A: Mom! The rice is mushy.
B: Oh, really? Maybe I put in too much water.

It's gone flat.
A: Hey, don't drink this coke. It's gone flat.
B: I don't mind. In fact, I prefer it.

The broth is refreshing!
A: The broth is refreshing!
B: In Korea, people drink this as a hangover remedy.

My noodles have gone soggy.
A: My noodles have gone soggy.
B: That's why you should talk less and eat.

Rice goes well with laver.
A: Rice goes well with laver. They're my favorite combination.
B: You should eat some meat, too.

The coffee is lukewarm.
A: Excuse me. The coffee is lukewarm.
B: Sorry, sir, I'll get you another one.
