# 일상생활 청소 & 빨래

The house is a total mess!
A: What? The house is a total mess!
B: Sorry, I'll clean it all up by tomorrow.

Did you vacuum?
A: Did you vacuum?
B: Yes, why? Did I miss something?

I'll wipe it with a wet rag.
A: I vacuumed, but the kitchen floor is still so dirty.
B: Really? Don't worry. I'll wipe it with a wet rag.

Take out the trash.
A: Are you going out to smoke? Take out the trash while you're at it.
B: I quit smoking.

Separate the trash.
A: No, no. Not like that. Separate the trash.
B: Sorry. I'm new at this.

Is this recyclable?
A: Excuse me, is this recyclable?
B: Your beer can? Of course!

Let's do the laundry.
A: Let's do the laundry.
B: That's a bad idea. It's going to rain soon.

Fold the laundry.
A: Where are you going? Fold the laundry.
B: Hold on. I need a break.

How do I get this stain out?
A: How do I get this stain out?
B: Oops, I don't think it will come out.

It shrank!
A: Oh, no! It shrank!
B: The water must've been too hot!
