# 우울 & 침체

You look down.
A: You look down. Is it your school grades?
B: Yes. I got two Fs this time.

I feel blue.
A: Helen? Why are you calling me at this hour?
B: Can you talk? I feel blue.

He's feeling under the weather today.
A: Is Mark home? I'd like to talk to him.
B: Sorry. He can't. He's feeling under the weather today.

What's eating you?
A: You are quiet nowadays. What's eating you?
B: My rent is overdue. I have to move out if I can't pay up.

Why the long face?
A: Why the long face? Do you want to talk?
B: My friend is getting married, but I don't even have a girlfriend.

I'm not in the mood.
A: How about going ice skating in Central Park?
B: Sorry. I'm not in the mood. I want to be alone.

I feel uneasy.
A: I feel uneasy. I'm betraying William.
B: No, it's okay. It's William who betrayed you first.

It hurts my ego.
A: Ask John for help. He'll lend you some money.
B: I can't. He used to work for me. It hurts my ego.

I'm not cross.
A: I don't think you should go and see Robert tonight.
B: Don't worry. I'm not cross with Robert.

You are pulling my leg!
A: Congratulations. You won the lottery.
B: What? No way! You are pulling my leg!
